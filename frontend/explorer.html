Policy history

Every verdict carries the SHA-256 of the policy JSON it was decided with. The PolicyRegistry announces each policy with its file; changes go through the Safe and a timelock, so they are public before they take effect. "Verify" downloads the file and re-hashes it here.

Governance

The registry admin and pauser roles belong to a Safe multisig: no single key can change roles, parameters or the policy. Owners sign pending Safe transactions here with their own wallet (gasless, EIP-712); anyone can execute once enough owners signed.
