    Architecture

    Principle: the blockchain does not detect forgery. AI / image forensics verify the artwork first; the smart contract only stores a tamper-proof record of the verdict and refuses to mint without a passing one.

    1. Preprocessing – validation, decompression-bomb guard, RGB normalisation, SHA-256 of the pixels.
    2. ResNet-18 – fully fine-tuned on 17k real-world images: authentic vs forged (AI-generated + manipulated).
    3. GAN / AI detector – linear probe on frozen CLIP features (generalises to unseen generators) + power-spectrum fingerprint.
    4. Copy-move – block matching with shift-vector voting; score = dominance of one displacement.
    5. Perceptual hash – 64-bit pHash, Hamming distance to every minted NFT.
    6. CLIP copy score – similarity to the protected collection, relative to each work's own nearest neighbour.
    7. ORB – keypoint matching with RANSAC homography on the CLIP/pHash shortlist.
    8. Policy engine – data-calibrated thresholds → per-check risk → weighted score → PASS / REVIEW / FAIL.
    9. MintGateNFT.sol – records every verdict (incl. failures), reviewer resolution, one token per image.
