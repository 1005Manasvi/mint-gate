    Architecture
    
      
    Principle: the blockchain does not detect forgery. AI / image forensics verify the artwork first;
        the smart contract only stores a tamper-proof record of the verdict and refuses to mint without a passing one.
    
      
      
        
    1. Wallet identity: Sign-In with Ethereum (EIP-4361); every upload is signed by the artist's wallet.
      2. 
        
    2. Preprocessing: validation, decompression-bomb guard, RGB normalisation, SHA-256 of the pixels.
      3. 
        
    3. Provenance: C2PA Content Credentials (signature chain verified), Stable Diffusion / SDXL invisible watermarks, generator metadata (A1111, ComfyUI, IPTC "trainedAlgorithmicMedia"); optional reverse image search (advisory).
      4. 
        
    4. AI detector: linear probes on frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 + SigLIP 2 B/16 features, chosen on an out-of-source benchmark of 2025-26 generators.
      5. 
        
    5. Manipulation forensics: TruFor localisation + reliability map (GPU profile; can flag for review, never fail).
      6. 
        
    6. Copy-move: block matching with shift-vector voting; score = dominance of one displacement.
      7. 
        
    7. Exact copy: PDQ hash (mirrored / rotated too) against protected works and 270k indexed Ethereum NFTs.
      8. 
        
    8. Edited copy: SSCD + DINOv2 descriptors with region crops, density-relative scoring; collection siblings are not copies.
      9. 
        
    9. Policy engine: data-calibrated thresholds → per-check risk → weighted score → PASS / REVIEW / FAIL.
      10. 
        
    10. MintGateRegistry + MintGateNFTV2: EIP-712 verdicts, one mint per image, artist-paid mints, disputes.
